# Blob — first five posts

Five vertical clips (1080×1920, 9–13 s, no music baked in). They're made for TikTok, Instagram Reels and
YouTube Shorts. Add a trending, quiet sound in the app when you post: lofi or soft piano fits Blob.

Post one every day or two, in this order. Same caption works on all three apps; trim hashtags on Instagram.

---

## 1. `blob-meet.mp4` — Meet Blob

> There’s a notch on your Mac. Something lives in it now.
> Blob is a little character that lives in your MacBook’s notch and does what you say. Free at blobnotch.app

`#macbook #macos #mac #productivity #desktopsetup #indiedev`

**First comment:** “Made this myself (I’m 14). Mac and Windows, free.” Only if you’re comfortable saying your age.

## 2. `blob-sayit.mp4` — Hold ⌥, say it

> Hold right Option. Say it. Let go. Blob turns your voice into text on your Mac and gets it done: opens apps, plays music, runs Shortcuts.

`#macbook #mactips #productivityhacks #ai #voiceassistant`

## 3. `blob-moods.mp4` — Six moods

> It never says a word. Idle, curious, happy, busy, alarmed, sleepy. Which one are you right now?

`#macbook #animation #cute #desktopbuddy #mac`

Ask people to comment their mood; it gets replies.

## 4. `blob-asks.mp4` — It asks first

> It asks before it deletes. Blob can do things on your Mac, so it stops and checks before deleting, sending or buying anything.

`#macbook #ai #aiagent #privacy #mac`

## 5. `blob-claude.mp4` — Claude Code buddy

> If you code with Claude Code: Blob shows every session in your notch, working, done or waiting on you, so you can look away.

`#claudecode #claude #coding #programming #developer #macbook`

## Tips

- **First second matters most.** All five clips open on something moving. Don't add an intro.
- **Reply to comments with a new video.** “Does it work on Windows?” → answer with a screen recording.
- **Real screen recordings beat everything.** Record Blob on your own Mac (Cmd-Shift-5), especially it reacting to music, and post those between these.
- **Bio:** `Blob lives in your notch 🫧 free for Mac + Windows → blobnotch.app`
- Don't buy followers or use engagement bots. Those apps hide your posts for it.
